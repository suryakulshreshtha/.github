# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## Our pledge

I want participation in these projects to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Give feedback on the work, with a reason. "This locator will break on a
  restyle, here is why" is useful; "this is bad" is not.
- Accept feedback on your own work the same way.
- Assume the other person knows something you do not.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, fix them, and move on.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal and political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **kulshreshtha.surya@engineer.com**. Reports
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's
privacy will be respected.

I may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that do not align with this code of conduct, and will explain the
reasoning when doing so.
